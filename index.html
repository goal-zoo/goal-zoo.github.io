Goal Zoo is a penalty shootout game for children: you take the shots, and a zoo of animal goalkeepers tries to stop them. It is built so that a child can play it safely, and that starts with a simple rule: the game collects nothing.

This page explains, in plain language, what Goal Zoo does — and doesn't do — with information.

The short version

  • The game doesn't collect, send, sell, or share any personal data — from children or from anyone else.
  • The game never connects to the internet. There are no servers, no accounts, and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking in the game, and no advertising or analytics code from anyone else.
  • What the game remembers, like the best score and the stickers, is kept on the device and is never sent to us.
  • The only personal data we ever receive is an email that a parent chooses to send us.

Who we are

Goal Zoo is made and published by an independent developer: the person shown as the seller (developer) on the game's page in the app store. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that developer. For anything about privacy, write to the address under Contact.

What the game remembers

To pick up where a child left off, Goal Zoo saves a few things on the device, in the app's own storage. None of it identifies anyone, and none of it ever reaches us.

Backups. The save is part of the app's data on the device. If the device's owner backs the device up or moves to a new one (for example with iCloud Backup, a backup on a computer, or the device backup of an Android phone), the save may be included in that backup. Such backups are made and controlled by the device's owner and their backup provider. We have no access to them.

No internet connection

Goal Zoo makes no network requests at all. Its pictures, animations, and sounds are created by the game itself, on the device, so there is nothing to download and nothing to send. It works the same in airplane mode.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting tools and no trackers. The only code in the game that we did not write ourselves is open-source software that draws the pictures and runs the game on the device (it is listed under Licences in the parents' area); it collects nothing and contacts no one. We don't build a profile of anyone, and we don't follow anyone across apps or websites. There is no data collection to opt out of, because there is no data collection.

Children's privacy

Goal Zoo is made for children and is designed to meet the app stores' rules for children's apps, such as the Kids category of the App Store. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either — in line with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States), the GDPR (European Union), and KVKK (Türkiye).

The game has no chat, no sharing, no social features, and no way to post or send anything.

The parents' area

The For Parents area in the game sits behind a parental gate: a multiplication question written out in words, whose answer has to be typed on a number pad. A wrong answer closes the gate for a while, longer each time. The area closes by itself when the game is left, when the privacy link is opened, and after a minute and a half without a touch.

It holds the sound and vibration switches, a summary of the child's progress, a button that resets the progress, the open-source licences, and the only thing in the game that leads outside it: the link to this page, which opens in the device's web browser.

Goal Zoo has no in-app purchases today. If we ever add any, they will be reachable only behind this parental gate and handled by the app store, and we will update this policy before they arrive.

App stores

Goal Zoo is distributed through app stores; today that is Apple's App Store. The store may give us aggregate, anonymous statistics, such as how many times the game was downloaded. If the device's owner has chosen to share diagnostics with app developers (on an iPhone: Share With App Developers in the device's settings), the store may also share anonymous crash reports with us, which we use only to fix bugs. None of this identifies a person, and it is handled under the store's own privacy policy, such as Apple's Privacy Policy.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The page itself has no analytics or tracking.

If you write to us

The contact address is for parents and other adults. Children should not write to us: if a child wants to tell us something, a parent can write for them.

When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message. We use them only to answer you and to fix the problem you describe. We don't add you to any list, we don't use your address for anything else, and we don't pass it on: it is handled only by us and by the email service that carries and stores our mail. We delete the correspondence within 12 months after the matter is settled, and sooner if you ask.

Please don't send us a child's name, photo or other personal details: we don't need them. The error note from the parents' area, if you choose to send it, contains only a date and a technical error text.

Deleting data

Reset progress in the parents' area erases the saved progress: the best score, the keepers met, the stickers and the totals. Deleting Goal Zoo from the device removes everything it saved, settings included. A copy may remain in an older device backup until the device's owner replaces or deletes that backup.

We never hold any game data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Emails you have sent us are deleted as described above, or right away if you ask.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU) and KVKK (Türkiye) give you rights over your personal data: to know what is held, to get a copy, and to have it corrected or erased. The game never gives us any personal data, so these rights can only concern an email you have sent us. Write to the contact address and we will show you what we have, correct it or delete it. We handle such emails because we have a legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us. If you think we have got something wrong, you can also complain to the data-protection authority where you live (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

Changes to this policy
