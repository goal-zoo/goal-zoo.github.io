Privacy Policy

No ads. No tracking. No internet. Just penalties.

Goal Zoo is a penalty shootout game for children: you take the shots, and a zoo of animal goalkeepers tries to stop them. It is built so that a child can play it safely, and that starts with a simple rule: the game collects nothing.

This page explains, in plain language, what Goal Zoo does — and doesn't do — with information.

The short version

  • We don't collect, store, sell, or share any personal data — from children or from anyone else.
  • The game never connects to the internet. There are no servers, no accounts, and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs in the game.
  • The few things the game remembers, like the best score, are kept only on the device and never leave it.

What the game remembers

To pick up where a child left off, Goal Zoo saves a handful of game settings on the device, in the app's own storage. None of it identifies anyone, and none of it ever reaches us.

No internet connection

Goal Zoo makes no network requests at all. Its pictures, animations, and sounds are created by the game itself, on the device, so there is nothing to download and nothing to send. It works the same in airplane mode.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting tools, no trackers, and no third-party code of any kind. We don't build a profile of anyone, and we don't follow anyone across apps or websites. There is no data collection to opt out of, because there is no data collection.

Children's privacy

Goal Zoo is made for children and is designed to meet the App Store's rules for the Kids category. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either — in line with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States), the GDPR (European Union), and KVKK (Türkiye).

The game has no chat, no sharing, no social features, and no way to post or send anything.

The parents' area

The For Parents area in the game sits behind a parental gate: a question that a young child can't easily answer. It holds the only thing in the game that leads outside it — the link to this page, which opens in the device's web browser.

Goal Zoo has no in-app purchases today. If we ever add any, they will be reachable only behind this parental gate and handled by Apple, and we will update this policy before they arrive.

The App Store

Goal Zoo is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may give us aggregate, anonymous statistics, such as how many times the game was downloaded. If the device owner has turned on Share With App Developers in the device's settings, Apple may also share anonymous crash reports with us, which we use only to fix bugs. None of this identifies a person, and it is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's Privacy Statement. The page itself has no analytics or tracking.

Deleting data

Deleting Goal Zoo from the device removes everything it saved. Since we never hold any data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU) and KVKK (Türkiye) give you rights over your personal data. Goal Zoo never receives any, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or erase — but you're always welcome to ask us anything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the effective date above and publish the new version at this same address before it takes effect.
