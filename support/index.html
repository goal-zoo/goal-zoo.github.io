How to play

  • Swipe from the ball toward the goal. Where your finger lifts off is where the ball goes.
  • Swipe in a curve to bend the ball around the goalkeeper. A quicker swipe makes a harder shot.
  • Start your swipe on the bottom of the ball to chip it over the keeper, or on the top to keep it low along the grass.
  • You have five lives, shown as hearts, and a miss costs one. Misses before your first goal of a game are a warm-up and cost nothing, and each time a new keeper walks in, one life comes back. Score as many goals as you can: your best score is shown on the main menu.
  • Every 4 goals a new goalkeeper takes over, eight in all, from Keeper Kaan to the fearless honey badger. Each one has a weak spot, and the tip on its card tells you how to beat it.
  • Tap a keeper you have met on the main menu to open its card: a new animal fact each time, and practice against it, with no lives and no record.
  • Special goals earn stickers for the sticker album on the main menu.

For parents

  • Goal Zoo has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never goes online. See the privacy policy.
  • The Parents area at the bottom of the main menu is behind a parental gate: a multiplication question written out in words. Type the answer on the number pad. A wrong answer closes the gate for a few seconds, longer each time. The area closes by itself when you leave the game, open the privacy policy, or don't touch it for a minute and a half.
  • In the parents' area you find the sound and vibration switches, a summary of your child's progress, Reset progress, the open-source licences, the privacy policy and the version number.
  • Progress (best score, keepers met, stickers) is stored only on the device. To start over, use Reset progress in the parents' area; deleting the app erases everything, settings included.
  • No sound? Check the sound button on the main menu and the sound switch in the parents' area, make sure the iPhone isn't in silent mode, and turn the volume up.
  • The game speaks 15 languages. It starts in the device's language if that is one of them, and in English otherwise. To change it, tap the globe button (🌐) on the main menu, or choose a language for Goal Zoo in the iPhone's Settings (Goal Zoo → Language).
  • Goal Zoo needs an iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.
