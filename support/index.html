How to play

  • Swipe from the ball toward the goal. Where your finger lifts off is where the ball goes.
  • Swipe in a curve to bend the ball around the goalkeeper. A quicker swipe makes a harder shot.
  • Start your swipe on the bottom of the ball to chip it over the keeper, or on the top to keep it low along the grass.
  • You can miss five times. Score as many goals as you can before then: your best score is shown on the main menu.
  • Every 4 goals a new goalkeeper takes over, eight in all, from Keeper Kaan to the fearless honey badger. Each one has a weak spot, and the tip on its card tells you how to beat it.

For parents

  • Goal Zoo has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never goes online. See the privacy policy.
  • The Parents area at the bottom of the main menu is behind a parental gate: a multiplication question written out in words. It has a short note about the game, the privacy policy and the version number.
  • Progress (best score and the keepers met) is stored only on the iPhone. To start over, delete the app and install it again.
  • No sound? Check the sound button at the bottom right of the main menu, make sure the iPhone isn't in silent mode, and turn the volume up.
  • The game follows the iPhone's language (English or Turkish). Switch with TR / EN on the main menu.
